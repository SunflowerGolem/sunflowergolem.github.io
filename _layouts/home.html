Hello there! I’m Tais, a game writer and narrative designer with 7+ years of experience on PC, console and mobile games. I build storylines and plots, design the narrative structure and pacing, and tie the story into the game’s mechanics. I also create quests and characters, write dialogue (branching and linear), lore and worldbuilding, item descriptions and other in-game text, and implement everything in the game engine.

What fascinates me most about storytelling is its ability to create genuine feeling. The story itself may not be real, but the emotions are. I think that’s something truly magical. I love creating immersive, interactive stories, and when players get lost in a story I made, that’s the best reward for me.

I feel incredibly fortunate to have worked on a range of different and amazing projects throughout my career. Most recently, I’ve been a narrative designer on THE LIFT: Supernatural Handyman Simulator, an eerie immersive sim published by tinyBuild, where I designed the plot structure, character arcs, lore and quests and implemented them in Unity.
I also wrote all the dialogue for two campaigns of Ablight, a classic RTS by Cynaps Arts, and worked on Project Rebirth, Space Aces and Mountain King.
Before that, I spent almost four years as the sole writer and narrative designer on Time of the Sword, an AAA action adventure game by Alpha Games Studio, where I was responsible for crafting not only the game’s plot and characters but also its world and lore based on Slavic folklore.
Earlier in my career, I wrote quests, characters and dialogue for Design Masters, a popular home decor mobile game by Playgendary.

My love for stories doesn’t stop at work. I’m a big fan of tabletop role-playing games, and I run my own D&D campaign, An Average Adventure. We’ve been playing it for six years now: my players started with level 1 characters, and today their heroes are level 15! The game is set in my own dark fantasy world, which is on the edge of war. The emperor is dead, a plague is turning people into the walking dead, and a demon cult has opened a gate to another world. I build the story around my players’ character backstories, and I give them hard choices where nobody is completely right. Running this campaign has taught me a lot about storytelling and worldbuilding, and about making quests, fights and mechanics that keep players on their toes.

I haven’t only written games, I’ve written about them too. I used to write and translate articles for GoodGame.ru, a cyber-sports and game news site, and I even had my own column about board games.
I was also a judge for the Narrative Design Awards.

Want to see how I write? Read my game writing samples. I’m currently open to full-time roles and freelance projects (learn more).

Let’s make some magic happen together!
