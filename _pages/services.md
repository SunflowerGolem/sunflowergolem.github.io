## For indie developers and small projects {#indie}

If you're making an indie game, a visual novel or a tabletop campaign and need a defined piece of writing, the simplest way is to order through Fiverr. My packages, prices, delivery times and client reviews are all on my gig.

Typical orders:

- Quests and side missions, including branching ones.
- Characters: backstories, motivations and distinct voices.
- Lore and worldbuilding: factions, history, myths, item text.
- Branching dialogue scenes and barks.
- Story outlines and plot structure.

### What I work on

- **Story and world:** pitches, outlines, story bibles, plot structure, character arcs, worldbuilding and lore.
- **Quests and missions:** main and side quests, branching mission sequences, and encounters written around the gameplay.
- **Dialogue:** branching and diamond-shape dialogue, distinct character voices, quest banter and barks.
- **Cinematics and trailers:** screenplay-format scripts for in-game cinematics, intros and marketing trailers.
- **In-game text:** item descriptions, flavor text, quest text and character bios.
- **Narrative systems:** story that ties into the mechanics, such as boss fights, abilities, puzzles and progression.
- **Implementation:** setting up narrative content in the engine (Unity, Unreal, articy:draft, Twine), preparing text for localization, and writing documentation for the team.
- **Narrative review:** feedback on an existing script, plot or game design document.

### Genres and formats

RPG, narrative adventure, visual novel, cozy, real-time strategy, action/slasher, immersive sim, match-3/casual, and tabletop (D&D 5e, Pathfinder and homebrew systems). Genres range from fantasy, sci-fi, horror and cyberpunk to detective, romance, drama and comedy. I have written novels and comics too.

I can deliver in whatever format your team uses: Google Docs, PDF, spreadsheets, or dialogue formatted for Twine, Ren'Py, Ink and Yarn Spinner.
