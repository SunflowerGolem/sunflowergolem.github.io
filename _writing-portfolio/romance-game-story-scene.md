[The Lust for Loot](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19E9H504PGBhGSw5_eCG6ueVunHRu-ood/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} - a choice-based interactive romance game writing sample in a fantasy setting. It shows my work with short diamond-shape choice trees, written with animation economy and laconic text as the priority.

Adele, a daring young woman thief, infiltrates a princess's room to pilfer her jewels, but her plans are upended when she becomes captivated by a golden bracelet adorned with a dragon. She puts it on only to be surprised by a man named Draco, who toys with Adele's desires.

What does Adele want more: to get out of the danger or to surrender to her lust for loot?

## Excerpt {#excerpt}

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

Adele is a young woman in a dark leather suit and a mask on her face. She waits in a shadow for a guard to pass, then deftly climbs the fence, goes on a roof, and jumps to the balcony rails.

The moon is full and bright. Adele sneaks to the door leading from the balcony to the room, picks the lock, and steps into the room.

It’s a richly-furnished bedroom. In the gap between the heavy bed curtains, Adele sees a figure lying in bed and seemingly sleeping at the moment. Adele quietly moves to the dressing table on the other side of the room.

The table is full of gems and jewelry. All of them are beautiful but there is one outstanding piece - a golden bracelet with a spread-winged dragon figure on it lying in the center of the table. Adele pauses in delight. She takes her mask off and picks up the bracelet carefully.

The moment she puts on the bracelet a figure appears behind her. She turns around holding her hand on a dagger sheath.

A man with long red hair and arms and neck covered in golden patterns chuckles. He’s not armed.

**Mysterious stranger:** Who do I see here? A thief? Don’t worry, I won’t call for guards… As long as you obey.

- Answer variant 1 – **Adele:** I won’t obey anyone!
- Answer variant 2 – **Adele:** I’ll do what you say...

**Mysterious stranger:** This attitude I like. So who are you, my night guest?
