[Something big, something blue](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aRQwfkTlqk_MX-KySEhCaDdYVo_dcaeq/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} - a female-targeted modern setting writing sample with light comedy and drama elements. It shows my work with dialogue and distinct character tones.

When a co-worker shows up to Sarah’s bridal shower with a provocative gift, a timid bride-to-be along with her friends and future family members struggle to handle the situation while dealing with conflicting personalities.

What is this big and blue thing after all?

## Excerpt {#excerpt}

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

Setting: A spacious living room in a suburban neighborhood.

Characters:

- Sarah, a 25-year-old anxious bride-to-be
- Rachel, Sarah's best friend and the host of the bridal shower, an event planner with a love for all things organized
- Lucy, Sarah's younger sister, a ditzy teenager and a social media influencer
- Maria, Sarah's co-worker and a bit of a drama queen, always eager to stir up trouble
- Karen, Sarah's future mother-in-law, a well-meaning but controlling woman
- Emily, Sarah's future sister-in-law, a bit harsh and eccentric art history student

As the scene opens, Sarah, Rachel and Karen are setting up for the bridal shower, decorating the living room with balloons, streamers, and an assortment of themed decorations. Lucy is recording a TikTok video. Emily is leaning against the wall, scowling and looking bored.

With a flourish of her designer coat and a flick of her perfectly-coiffed hair Maria enters in a perfume cloud, on high heels and accompanied by two movers carrying a huge gift wrapped in sparkly paper.

**Maria:** (bounding into the room) Hey, everyone! Sorry I'm late. Traffic was a nightmare.

**Rachel:** (smiling) No problem, Maria. We're just glad you could make it.

**Maria:** (eyebrows raised) Oh, I wouldn't miss Sarah's bridal shower for the world. (pauses dramatically) I have the most amazing gift for her!

**Sarah:** (unsure) Really? I… I can't wait to see what it is.
