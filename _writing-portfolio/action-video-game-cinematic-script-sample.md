FADE IN: EXT. JUNGLE EDGE - DAY

Trees loom overhead, their foliage casting dappled shadows. The sounds of wildlife create a symphony of chirping birds and buzzing insects.

CALEB "THUNDER" STORM – the leader of the group, a deliberate man in his thirties steps forward cautiously. His long, black hair is tied back in a braid.

Caleb crouches behind vegetation, observing the scene before him. A fortified and guarded compound nests in the valley next to the river. Upstream lies a reservoir created by a low dam across. Pipes connect it to the compound.

Meanwhile there is a silent movement behind him. A snake with scales of earthy and olive tones slithers closer from a tree branch, closing in on its unsuspecting prey.

The snake lunges forward, baring its fangs, when a hand swiftly appears from the side, seizing the snake's head with a firm grip.

NIA "HARPY" THOMPSON – a tall black woman, with a shaved head adorned with tattoos – fights the snake, matching its rage.

**NIA:** Kill it!

The sudden commotion catches attention of the guards and Caleb ducks down, pulling Nia with him, seeking cover.

He reaches for his gun, his gaze shifting between the snake and the guards. Nia holds the snake pinned to a sturdy tree root, struggling to restrain its wriggling body, while Caleb hesitates, aware that firing a shot could draw unwanted attention.

A swift, precise and quiet strike with a butt of a rifle incapacitates the snake as another figure emerges from the shadows.

ETHAN "SERAPH" ROBERTS – a subtle white man in his forties, with salt and pepper hair – grabs the stunned snake, tosses it away from the group and immediately moves towards Nia, inspecting her arms for any signs of injury.

**ETHAN:** A Fer-de-Lance, has neurotoxic highly potent venom. Did it bite?

Nia abruptly pulls her hand away and rises from the ground.

**NIA:** I’m fine. You should’ve killed it.

**ETHAN:** It’s just a living creature trying to survive.
