In the steampunk city of Gearhaven, advanced machinery unexpectedly turns into perilous mechanisms, disrupting the once-thriving industrial society. Locals dub it the Shadow Curse, yet its essence and origin remain a mystery. The main hero, a daring and ambitious airship captain and smuggler, discovers that he unknowingly transported some artifact - a part of machinery that might be connected to the Shadow Curse.
From this point, his adventures have only begun.

## Excerpt {#excerpt}

The sample presents the same story four ways: an outline, a synopsis, a three-act structure and a hero's journey. Here are the synopsis and the first act.

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Synopsis**

In a steampunk city of Gearhaven a Shadow Curse occurs - advanced machinery twists into perilous mechanisms. A daring airship captain and smuggler unknowingly carries an artifact linked to this mystery during a smuggling gig gone south.

When he figures out he can use the Curse's power, the captain dreams of becoming a rich and famous Gearhaven's hero. By collecting fragments from hostile machinery, he boosts his own abilities. Finally, he finds out that the city's bigwigs purposefully unleashed the Shadow Curse.

He manages to destroy the Curse completely, but it comes at a cost - he has to give up his newfound power and live on the run. Getting back his ship, he sails away into the steamed sunset, leaving a city as a hero who nobody knows existed.

**Three Act Structure. Act 1: Setup**

- Introduction to Gearhaven, a steampunk city facing a mysterious crisis known as the Shadow Curse.
- Advanced machinery transforms into dangerous mechanisms, disrupting the once-thriving industrial society.
- The protagonist, an ambitious airship captain and smuggler, unknowingly transports an artifact connected to the Shadow Curse during a smuggling operation.
- Desire for wealth, fame, and heroism fuels the captain's aspirations.
