[This dialogue](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dlX6_l7HE9S6YCeBGRwRqzg27C9llEPe/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} showcases a tense interaction between a playable character and an NPC. A playable character confesses his feelings for his friend's sister, prompting various emotional responses from the NPC that reflect her protective instincts. The player navigates through choices that explore themes of love, danger, and autonomy. This diamond-shaped dialogue mirrors the rich, choice-driven interactions typical in Baldur's Gate 3 and implies different developments of the situation in the future depending on the answers.

## Example {#example}

The opening node of the dialogue: one NPC line, three player options, and a distinct response to each before the branches converge.

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Io:** Hells, what's up with this serious face, Clinton? I haven't seen it since that time when hydra swallowed me and you had to fight all five heads to cut it open.

Player’s dialogue options:

1. Don't get angry, Io, but I think I like your sister.
2. I like your sister. And she likes me too. I thought you should know.
3. Your sister's with me now. Any trouble with that?

Io's responses:

1. **Io:** Don't get angry? Well, I am furious!
2. **Io:** I knew already. Only the blind worm wouldn't notice.
3. **Io:** Actually, yeah. I do have one little issue...

</div>
