Little did he know, this discovery would lead him to uncover the lair of a fearsome black dragon and ultimately reveal his own hidden strength and courage.

## Excerpt {#excerpt}

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

Where legends whispered through the leaves and magic danced upon the winds there lived a clever halfling named Rid.

In his journey he passed through villages where stories of vanished people filled the air. Many embarked on brief adventures and errands, only to disappear without a trace.

As daylight dipped below the horizon, a golden glow enveloped the village square bustling with people folding their tents for the night, when Rid's gaze fell upon a peculiar sight - a weathered parchment lying near the roots of a tree. Intrigued, he approached and retrieved the parchment, its surface adorned with symbols and faded ink. It was a treasure map, promising riches beyond imagination.

Rid inquired about the merchant who had occupied this spot during the day. Villagers mentioned that he hailed from the mountainside, traded in various knickknacks, weapons, and armor used in battle and went back the same direction.

"Well now, isn't this a curious turn of events?" Rid chuckled to himself. "Here I am, stumbling upon a treasure map in the midst of my travels. But what need have I for riches?" He paused, scratching his chin. "But the merchant... now there's a fellow who could put such a map to good use. Money, after all, hold much allure for those who trade in knickknacks and trinkets. He must have lost the map. And who am I to deny him such an opportunity?"
