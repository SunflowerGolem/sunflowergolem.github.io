<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

EXT. THE DAM - DAY

John jumps over the railing and starts sliding down the dam. Over-the-shoulder view. The slope of the dam initially allows for some sliding, but it gradually gets steeper. As John slides down next to the rift line, he chops at the tentacles protruding from the crack, writhing as they try to crush the dam. His ally, who stays at the top, starts an incantation.

The slide accelerates. Sparks fly as the steel of John’s suit scrapes against the concrete. The frenzied tentacles break chunks off the dam, sending fragments flying in all directions. John fiercely slashes at every monster appendage he can reach. Eventually, he passes the entire length of the rift, leaving the tentacles behind him.

At some point, the slide turns into a free fall as the dam becomes completely vertical. The camera shows this from above and follows him through the falling debris. John spreads his arms and legs wide to slow his descent. Chunks of the dam tumble around him, spinning deceptively slow at varying speeds.

Wide shot of the dam. John dodges and maneuvers through the debris. Chaos reigns around him. Cracks run along the dam, closing and opening again. Jets of water burst through, then disappear. Debris also crashes into the dam around John, embedding into the concrete with loud thuds.
