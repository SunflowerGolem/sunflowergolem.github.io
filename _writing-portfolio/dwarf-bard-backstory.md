[The dwarf bard backstory](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BsVojv88Vm5jYXYzDCCSVtN56gM9EwAs/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} is a concise piece where I build a promising foundation for a character to explore in tabletop role-playing games like DnD. I focused on providing a player with a wide range of possible personal characteristics to choose from while also offering a framework for future character exploration, depth, and the basis for their personal quest, all presented in a laconic form.

The backstory for the dwarf bard character is crafted as the initiation of their hero's journey arc, signifying the moment of their call to adventure and providing them with the motivation to embark on their quest, regardless of the campaign's theme or events.

## Excerpt {#excerpt}

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

The night before the big premiere, Russell Ironforge, lead bard of the traveling circus band, strolled purposefully through the circus camp on the outskirts of a new town they reached that afternoon.

Magni, a new bard in the band, had been sick-worried all day. 'Not every day you perform at your first premiere,' Russell decided. And any dwarf worth their ale knows that there's nothing quite like a spirited booze-up to soothe frayed nerves.

Russell hummed around Magni’s tent, opening the curtains with one swift motion. Bottles in hand, he suddenly stopped. The fabric of reality shivered as dark-cloaked figures held Magni. In a fleeting moment, Magni's eyes met Russell's with horror and prayer.

"Hey, you pile of griffinshit, leave him alone!" Russell dropped the bottles, catching the nearest figure by the cloak. It smoked and shifted starting to fade. Magni tried to scream through his gag. His body became transparent.

</div>
