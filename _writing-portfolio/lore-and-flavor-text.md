These short and punchy [lore and flavor text samples](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1L1_qM04kM6Mt0LvGxq4M1dlUx9ltvlEZ/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} show how I aim to make players feel more relaxed and connected to the game's world, all within 250 characters or less. Overall, the use of funny and concise lore and flavor texts in games can enhance the player's experience, making it more enjoyable and memorable. They also may be used in marketing materials where brevity and memorability are important.

## Examples {#examples}

Three of the items from the sample. Each item has two short texts, a lore line and a flavor line.

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Dagger Potion**

A seemingly ordinary vial of potion, but upon closer inspection, the lid is revealed to be a handle, and there is a sharp dagger blade hidden by liquid. The perfect tool for those looking to add an element of surprise to their next adventure.

The liquid in the vial looks like water, smells like water, and flows like water. All true signs of a Dagger Potion.

**The talking pouch**

Looking for some sage advice? Look no further, just untie the mouth of this pouch. Speaking speed’s up to two thousand words per minute! Though, no sense is guaranteed.

A soft muttering comes from the bag and the gem twinkles in a way that suspiciously resembles the dots and dashes of the Morse alphabet.

**Levitation platform**

Carrying capacity - up to five boulders. Surface material - artificial turf. Kick to launch.

The classic puzzle for the first grade magic art students: “What levitates in this case: the platform or the boulders?”
