[Zombie Zest](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HDQ6Z9xsPKP3GgHTSqCsSLeZUPoOwjAQ/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} is a sample showcasing the creative use of character barks in a zombie survival comedy game. This text presents a game similar to Left 4 Dead, featuring three characters with unique personalities and histories, navigating through a zombie apocalypse. This sample showcases how character barks can enrich the gaming experience, highlight the unique traits of each character, and add a layer of fun to challenging situations.

The sample is divided into three sections: brief bios of the three characters, their general barks, and a mission sequence that includes both a short cutscene dialogue and mission-specific barks.

## Examples {#examples}

The three characters and some of their generic barks.

<div class="sample-excerpt" markdown="1">

**Ruby Rotten** (68, British). A punk at heart, Ruby is as fierce as she was in her youth. A former punk rock band drummer, Ruby's sharp wit and no-nonsense attitude make her a formidable companion in any fight.

**Alex "Axle" Garcia** (17, Mexican). Alex grew up seeking adrenaline. He was a star in a roller derby arena often acting first and thinking later. The apocalypse didn't change him, just gave him a bigger playground. It may be his last ride, but he’s having fun.

**Master Nori** (43, Japanese). Having spent his life perfecting the art of sushi, Master Nori sees culinary skills as a form of care for others. He makes sure everyone is not only fed physically but also spiritually. His calm approach sets him apart in a world overrun by chaos.

| Trigger | Ruby Rotten | Alex "Axle" Garcia | Master Nori |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spotting enemies | Ah, fresh meat for the grinder! Time to rock your rotting socks off! | Hey, guys! Looks like the freak show's in town. | Unwanted guests approach. Let us welcome them properly. |
| Victory or escape | Punk's not dead! | One hell of a ride! Who's up for another lap? | Like a sharp knife through soft tofu. |
| Reviving or assisting teammates | Hang on, it's not time for your solo exit yet! | Thought you could sneak a break, huh? Let's get you back on track. | Every chef needs a sous-chef. Let me be yours at this moment. |
| Finding items or resources | Found some goodies! Time to turn up the volume! | Jackpot! This'll keep us rolling. | A fine addition to our pantry. |

</div>
